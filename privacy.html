ADMIRALES SYSTEM — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Policy explains how ADMIRALES SYSTEM, operated by the ADMIRALES team, may process information when you use the Discord bot. The exact information processed depends on enabled modules and configuration.

1. Information that may be processed

Depending on features and configuration, the application may process:

  • Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs to identify users and perform configured functions.
  • Usernames, display names, and message or interaction content where a feature requires it, such as moderation, logging, or request handling.
  • Moderation, verification, security, and command records generated when corresponding features are used.
  • Configuration values selected by server administrators.

We do not ask users for Discord passwords or account tokens.

2. Purposes

Information may be used to operate requested commands and server-management features; provide enabled moderation, security, verification, logging, backup, or status features; diagnose errors; prevent abuse; maintain security; and respond to support requests.

3. Storage and retention

Some features may store configuration or operational records in the operator's database or hosting environment. Retention depends on the feature and server configuration. Before relying on this Policy, the operator must specify the actual storage location, retention periods, and deletion process for each feature that stores data.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and infrastructure providers needed to operate the application. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect users or the application.

5. Choices and requests

You can stop using the application by removing it from your server. For privacy questions or requests, contact the ADMIRALES team through the support/contact method listed on the ADMIRALES community server. Server administrators may be able to remove information through available bot commands or configuration, where supported.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information processed by the application. No system or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

The application is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law or Discord's age requirements.

8. International transfers

Discord and infrastructure providers may process information in countries other than your own. Applicable safeguards depend on the services used.

9. Changes

Updates will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the ADMIRALES team through the support/contact method listed on the ADMIRALES community server.
